Why SpeakStrong?

Built by a long-time Toastmaster who wanted a better way to practise between meetings.

SpeakStrong started as a simple Table Topics tool and is evolving into a complete practice platform for Toastmasters members, evaluators and aspiring speakers.

Whether you're preparing for your Ice Breaker, a club speech, or the World Championship stage, SpeakStrong helps you practise with purpose.
